Geu Hoesa Geu Saram Uploader

"그 회사 그 사람 업로더" (Geu Hoesa Geu Saram Uploader) is an internal tool the channel operator uses to upload and manage videos they produce on their own channel. It is not offered to the public and is used only with the operator's own account and channel.
